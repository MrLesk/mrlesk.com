# Automations

<!--
⏱ 0:06. One minute.

Some sites need fresh data every day. You do not have to do that by hand.

[click] Open your Site's Automations and select Create schedule. You write when it runs and what it does, in plain words. Then you confirm it. While you build, ChatGPT may also suggest "Set up scheduled task".

[click] From then on it runs by itself. Every morning at 08:00, new guests show up on the map.

It runs in the cloud, so your laptop can be closed. You can edit, pause or resume a schedule under Scheduled, and a Site can have more than one.

One limit: a schedule can't use app access that a visitor approved on your Site. It works with the Site's own database or another supported data connection.

The guest-list example is ours. The docs give no example. Schedules are for a published Site, and they came out on September 29, 2026.

Source: help.openai.com, "Creating and using ChatGPT Sites", section "Schedule updates to a Site" (checked 2026-11-25).
