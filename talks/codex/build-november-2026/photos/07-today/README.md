# Today's pictures

Put the pictures and clips of 28 November 2026 here. They appear on the "Today" step of the year
in review, on the picture wall behind "Thank you for being part of this community" and on the
strip of the last slide. Files are shown in the order of their names.

The quickest way, from the deck's folder:

```bash
bun run photos ~/Downloads/today
```

That copies every picture and clip of that folder into this one, made smaller for the projector.
See "Add today's pictures" in the deck's README.
